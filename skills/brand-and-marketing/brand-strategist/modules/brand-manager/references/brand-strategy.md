# Brand Strategy Reference

Read this for Mode 1 (Strategist) and Mode 2 (Marketer).

## Positioning statement (fill this in)

> For **[audience]** who **[need/struggle]**, **[brand]** is the **[category]**
> that **[unique benefit]**. Unlike **[alternative]**, we **[key difference]**.

Keep it to one sentence. If you can't fill every blank with something specific,
the positioning isn't done — that's the first job.

## Audience personas (build 2-3)

Each persona needs:
- **Who** — role/life stage, location, income band
- **Wants** — the outcome they're chasing
- **Pains** — what's blocking them now
- **Where** — which platforms they actually use
- **Trigger** — what makes them act (a moment, an event, a feeling)

Don't build a persona you can't name a real example of. Vague personas produce
vague content.

## Content pillars (pick 3-5)

Pillars are repeatable themes every post maps to. A strong default mix:
1. **Education** — teach something useful (builds authority + saves/shares)
2. **Behind-the-scenes** — process, people, craft (builds trust + relatability)
3. **Social proof** — results, testimonials, case studies (builds belief)
4. **Offers** — what's for sale and how to get it (drives revenue)
5. **Engagement/Story** — personal, opinionated, interactive (drives reach)

Rule of thumb: ~80% value (1-3, 5), ~20% selling (4). All-offers kills reach.

## Voice spec

Define voice as **3 adjectives + 1 anti-pattern**:
- "We sound **warm, sharp, and confident**."
- "We **never** sound corporate or salesy."

Then give 2 example lines: one on-voice, one off-voice. Every piece of copy gets
checked against this.

## Competitive angle

In one paragraph: who are the 2-3 alternatives the audience considers, and what's
the *one* thing you do that they don't? If the honest answer is "nothing yet,"
that's the strategic problem to solve before scaling — say so.

## How to use the tools here

- Run `brand_health_scorer.py` with your honest 0-10 ratings → the bottom 3
  dimensions become your strategy priorities.
- Feed the pillars you choose into `content_calendar.py` to operationalize them.
