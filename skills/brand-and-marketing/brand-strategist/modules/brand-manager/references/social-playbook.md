# Social Playbook Reference

Read this for Mode 3 (Social Media Manager). Benchmarks are directional industry
medians — treat as a starting line, not a verdict. The real benchmark is your own
trend over time (re-run `engagement_analyzer.py` every 2 weeks).

## Platform cheat sheet

| Platform | Best-performing formats | Sustainable cadence | Primary growth lever |
|----------|------------------------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Instagram | Reels, carousels | 4-7 / week | Reels reach + saves/shares |
| TikTok | Native talking-head, tutorials, trends | 5-10 / week (volume game) | Watch-time + completion rate |
| X / Twitter | Threads, hot takes, replies | Daily + reply often | Replies in others' threads |
| LinkedIn | Text posts, document carousels, case studies | 3-5 / week | Comments in first hour |
| YouTube | Long-form + Shorts | 1-2 long / week, daily Shorts | Click-through + retention |
| Facebook | Photo posts, community/groups | 3-5 / week | Groups + shares |

## Engagement-rate benchmarks (% of reach)

- Instagram ~1.2% · TikTok ~2.5% · X ~0.5% · LinkedIn ~2.0% · Facebook ~0.6% · YouTube ~1.5%

These mirror the defaults in `engagement_analyzer.py`. Above benchmark = double
down on that format/pillar; well below = rework or drop it.

## Posting rhythm

- **Consistency beats volume.** 4 good posts/week every week > 14 posts then silence.
- **Batch.** Produce a full week in one session; schedule it.
- **Best times** vary by audience — start with weekday late-morning and early
  evening, then let your own analytics correct it. Don't trust generic "best time"
  charts over your export data.

## Community management

- **Response SLA:** reply to comments/DMs within a few hours while a post is live —
  early engagement compounds reach.
- **Engagement loops:** end posts with a question; reply to every early comment;
  spend 15 min/day commenting on adjacent creators' posts (especially on X/LinkedIn).
- **Growth loops:** collaborations, duets/stitches, reposting UGC, cross-posting
  to the platform where your audience is densest.

## Growth tactics that actually move the needle

1. **Hook quality** — the first 1-2 seconds / first line decides everything.
2. **Saves & shares > likes** — design posts people send to a friend or bookmark.
3. **Series** — recurring formats train the audience to come back.
4. **Repurpose** — one idea → Reel + carousel + thread + LinkedIn post.

## Hard rule

Don't recommend buying followers, engagement pods that violate ToS, or scrapers.
Growth is earned through format + consistency + community, and measured from the
user's own exported analytics.
