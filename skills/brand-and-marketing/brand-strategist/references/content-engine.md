# The Content Engine

Turns strategy into what gets published. Two modes:

- **Mode A: Build the system** (once per client): pillars, mix, platforms,
  cadence, formats, metrics.
- **Mode B: Plan the week** (recurring): Monny brings raw ideas, the engine returns
  context briefs. This is the most-used mode. Keep it fast.

Every piece must pass the Google Test (`personality.md`) and fit the
Personality Card.

---

## Mode B: Plan the week (use this fast path)

**Input from Monny:** client name (or "me"), a list of rough ideas, optionally the
shoot day or platform.

**Steps:**
1. Load the client's Personality Card and pillars if they exist (from a previous
   deliverable or memory). If they don't exist, ask Monny the 3 fast questions
   below instead of building the whole strategy.
2. Run each idea through the **Topic Filter**:
   - Does it make them known for their core thing (their one word / known FOR)?
   - Does it carry their belief, delivery, or world?
   - Is it genuinely useful to the one person they serve (not just views)?
   - Does it pass the Google Test? If not, which human layer fixes it?
3. Tag each idea: pillar, type (Deep / Nichewide / Personal), and job
   (teach a principle, show proof, or reveal a process).
4. Balance the week against the mix. Flag gaps ("no proof content this week").
5. Output **context briefs**, not scripts.

**3 fast questions** (only when no card exists):
1. Who is the one person watching, and what do they want right now?
2. What does this person believe that others in their field don't?
3. What happened recently (a client win, a lesson, a mistake) worth telling?

### The Context Brief (the output format, one per video)

```
#   TITLE (working)          Pillar · Type · Job · Platform
HOOK DIRECTION   The tension or question to open on (a direction, not a line)
THE POINT        One sentence: what they should walk away believing
THINK ABOUT      2-4 prompts that pull out THEIR story, opinion, or proof
                 e.g. "the client who almost quit in month two", "why you stopped
                 doing X"
WHY IT'S YOURS   The human layer that makes this un-Googleable
AVOID            The generic version of this topic
CLOSE            Where it points next (follow, comment prompt, lead magnet, offer)
```

Rules: never write full scripted lines unless Monny explicitly asks for a script.
Give territory, not text. Keep each brief under ~120 words.

A week view goes in one table first (day, title, pillar, type, platform, time
in the audience's timezone), then the briefs.

## Mode A: Build the system

### 1. Pillars (3-5)
Derive from the positioning's messaging pillars. Each pillar = a reason to
believe the claim. Name them in the client's vocabulary.

### 2. The mix

Stage-based starting point (adapted from Caleb Ralston's ratios; adjust to the
client):

| Stage | Deep (proves expertise) | Nichewide (discovery) | Personal (connection) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starting / under 10K | 70% | 20% | 10% |
| Growing | 65% | 25% | 10% |

For two-brand architectures use Monny's split (Client C model): 40% personal
account solo, 40% collab (personal + business accounts together), 20% business
solo (pure offer).

Once something works, run **70 / 20 / 10**: 70% proven formats, 20% iterations on
them, 10% experiments ("we love a good flop").

### 3. Formats (pick 2-3 signature formats)
- Deep: framework explainers, case studies with real numbers, process breakdowns,
  a long "manifesto" piece.
- Nichewide: myths vs truth, reactions, tier lists, one problem one fix.
- Personal: story time, behind the scenes, day in the work, lessons from failure.
- Carousels (LinkedIn / Instagram): one idea, one slide per beat, the person's own
  framework on the final slide.

### 4. The waterfall (one source, many pieces)
1. One pillar piece (long video, podcast, live, talk, sermon, or long post).
2. Mine 5-10 standout moments that work alone.
3. Repackage each natively per platform (clip, carousel, LinkedIn post, quote,
   newsletter). Not copy-paste. Pair with `video-clipper` for long recordings.

### 5. Platforms
One primary platform (where the ideal audience already is), one secondary.
Add a third only when the system is reliable. Rotate focus: one platform gets
new experiments for ~8 weeks, others run in maintenance mode.

### 6. Cadence
Start sustainable: one pillar piece per week or every two weeks, mined into
4-6 short pieces. Consistency beats perfection. Batch at most a month ahead so
feedback stays short. Build approval checkpoints: raw → edited → approved →
posted.

### 7. Metrics (only what changes decisions)
- Input: pieces published vs planned.
- Quality: watch time / average view duration, shares, saves.
- Trust: DMs and inquiries, judged by WHO sends them; lead magnet conversions.
- Multiplier: grade each post against the 90-day average (e.g. 2.4x). Outliers
  become hypotheses. Kill a format after three sub-1x results unless they love it.

### 8. Personal brand vs business brand content
- **Personal brand:** the person's voice, story, opinions. Face on camera.
- **Business brand:** proof, process, client results, offer clarity. Can be
  faceless but still opinionated. Borrows authority from the founder.
- **Both:** the founder is the front door, the business is the room people walk
  into.
