# Monetization: from trust to transaction

Extends section 4.4 of `strategy-playbook.md`. Load this for offer design,
pricing, and "how does this brand make money" questions.

## Principles (council law)

1. **Trust precedes the transaction.** Monetize trust, not attention.
2. **The bridge rule:** free content solves one problem and reveals a bigger one;
   the offer solves the bigger one. If free content and offer are unrelated, the
   ladder is broken.
3. **Price the value, not the hours.** If price is the objection, positioning is
   usually the real problem. Deliver more value rather than discounting. No
   apologetic price language.
4. **Sell privately first.** Early on, sell one-to-one to warm people with an
   existing offer. Public launches come after proof.
5. **Protect future you.** Refuse money that conflicts with the known-FOR / known-
   AGAINST associations (bad endorsements, off-brand sponsorships).
6. **Knowledge over sponsorship.** Expert brands earn most from services,
   education, and speaking, not brand deals.

## The ladder by brand type

| Rung | Personal brand (coach, minister, speaker) | Business brand (company, studio, product) |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Content, live sessions, lead magnet (guide, checklist, mini-training) | Content, case studies, free tool or audit |
| Entry | Workshop, small group, e-book, masterclass | Paid audit, starter package, trial |
| Core | Signature program or 1:1 coaching package | Main service or product |
| Premium | High-touch mentorship, retainer, mastermind | Retainer, enterprise, done-for-you |
| Leverage | Book, speaking, academy, licensing the method | Productized service, licensing, partnerships |

Build the Core offer first. Free and Entry exist to feed it. Premium exists to
anchor it.

## Pricing method

1. **Floor:** cost of delivery + minimum viable rate (what they need to earn).
2. **Value:** what the result is worth to the buyer (time saved, revenue, reach,
   peace, legacy). Price as a fraction of that.
3. **Anchor:** show the premium tier first so the core reads as reasonable.
4. **Currency and market:** price for where the buyer is, not where the expert
   is (a US-majority audience is not priced like a Nairobi one).
5. **Raise when:** a waitlist forms, close rate is high, or the calendar is full.

## Offer statement

"For [one person] who [pain], [offer name] delivers [result] in [timeframe]
through [method], so they can [bigger outcome]."

## Timing (with `sequencing.md`)
- Stage 0-1: one Core offer, sold privately. Optional Entry offer.
- Stage 2: lead magnet + email, public Core offer, Premium by invitation.
- Stage 3+: Leverage rung (book, speaking, academy).
