# Phase 2 — Discovery Questionnaire Bank

The questionnaire is the **mirror**: it shows the client what you heard and asks
for what's missing. It is always tailored to the gaps Phase 1 exposed. Never ship
a generic form.

---

## Tailoring logic (gap -> questions)

For every gap in the Gaps Table, pull the matching questions below. A 3-gap brand
typically yields an 18-30 question set.

| If the gap is... | Pull from sections... |
|---|---|
| Positioning | Difference & Belief, Audience |
| Language / voice | Voice, Identity & Story |
| Visibility | Visibility & Fear, Vision |
| Visual incoherence | Visual World (expand to full Visual Discovery) |
| Offer / monetization | Vision & Monetization |
| Audience confusion | Audience |

---

## The question bank

Conversational tone. Sounds like a real person asking, not a corporate intake.

### 1. Identity & Story
- Tell me the moment your work started to feel like *yours*.
- Who are you when nobody is watching?
- What did you do before this, and what did it teach you?
- What's a belief about your field that most people in it would disagree with?
- If your brand disappeared tomorrow, what would the right people miss most?

### 2. Audience (the one person)
- Describe the single person you most want to reach. Not a demographic, a person.
- What keeps that person up at night that your work speaks to?
- When they buy from you, what are they *actually* buying? (rarely the product)
- Who do you NOT want as an audience?
- Where does this person already spend their attention?

### 3. Difference & Belief
- What can you say that your closest competitor cannot credibly say?
- What do you stand *against*? (the enemy sharpens the position)
- Finish this: "Most people in my space think X, but I believe Y."
- What's the one thing you'd want to be known for owning?

### 4. Voice
- Drop 3 phrases or sentences that sound exactly like you talking.
- What words or tones make you cringe when brands use them?
- Are you more teacher, challenger, guide, or friend? Why?
- Formal or casual? Where's your line?

### 5. Visual World
- Three colours you're drawn to. Three you'd never use.
- Show me two brands whose look you admire (and what specifically).
- Clean and minimal, or rich and expressive? Where on that scale?
- What feeling should someone get in the first second of seeing your brand?
- Type: classic/serif, modern/sans, or a mix? Any fonts you love?

### 6. Visibility & Fear
- What's actually stopped you from showing up consistently?
- What are you afraid people will think if you claim your space loudly?
- How often can you realistically create, honestly?
- What's one thing you've wanted to say publicly but haven't?

### 7. Vision & Monetization
- Where is this brand in three years?
- What do you sell now, and what would you love to sell next?
- What would "this worked" look like, specifically?
- Free -> paid: what could you give away, and what's worth paying for?

## Brand Visual Discovery (when section 5 needs to go deep)

For visual-led clients (fashion, photography, design brands), expand the Visual
World section into a standalone interactive **Brand Visual Discovery** with these
modules and interactive elements:

- **Personal identity** — short answer boxes
- **Colour world** — choice grid of swatches + "never use" picks
- **Mood & aesthetic** — tag selection (e.g. cinematic, editorial, raw/film,
  Afro-Futuristic, clean, bold, organic)
- **Typography** — sample-pair picker (serif vs sans feel)
- **References** — reference grid, two-column "what I love / why"
- **Content visual direction** — sliding scale bars (minimal <-> expressive,
  calm <-> loud, classic <-> experimental)

---

## Interactive HTML pattern

Deliver digital questionnaires as a self-contained HTML widget with:
- Dark editorial aesthetic (grain texture, gold accents, serif headers) for the
  premium feel, OR light theme for plain intake.
- **Fillable form** with progress tracking.
- **Tag-selection** for traits and visual styles (multi-select pills).
- **Sliding scale bars** for spectrum questions.
- **Copy-to-clipboard export**: outputs either the completed answers as a
  formatted brief, or a blank version to send via DM.
- No browser storage APIs (won't work in the canvas) — keep state in memory.
- Fonts: Garamond/Lufga or Raleway/Montserrat. Never Inter/DM Sans.

For an in-person Day 1 session, deliver instead as a **landscape PDF** with choice
grids, sliding scale bars, answer boxes, and two-column layouts (ReportLab, black
and gold).

---

## Delivery rules
- 18-30 questions working range.
- Frame the opening as a mirror: "Here's what we heard. Now help us go deeper."
- After delivery, **STOP**. Strategy waits for answers. This is the human gate.
