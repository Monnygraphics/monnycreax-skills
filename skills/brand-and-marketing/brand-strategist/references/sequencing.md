# Sequencing: what to build, and when

Most brands fail on order, not effort. They build a website before they have a
claim, write a book before they have an audience, or hire before they have a
system. This module answers "when is the right time to ..." and produces short and
long-term goals.

Start with Caleb Ralston's Brand Journey to set direction, then place the client on
the stage ladder.

## Step 1: Brand Journey (direction)

| Question | Answer for this client |
|---|---|
| **Outcome:** what is this brand for in 3 years? (clients, speaking, book, sale of business, ministry reach) | |
| **Reputation:** what must they be known for to get it? | |
| **Actions:** what must they consistently do to earn that reputation? | |
| **Learning:** what skills or support do they need to do those actions? | |

The Outcome becomes the long-term goal. The Actions become the short-term goals.

## Step 2: The stage ladder

Find the stage from evidence, not ambition. Never skip a stage's gate.

| Stage | Name | Focus | Gate to move on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Clarity** | CORE Clarity + Ownership: who, for whom, the claim, Personality Card | The person can say the claim in one sentence and it passes the Google Test |
| 1 | **Proof** | One primary platform, consistent deep content, first case studies, private selling | 8-12 weeks of consistent publishing + at least 3 documented results |
| 2 | **Trust** | Waterfall system, a lead magnet, email list, a signature offer, the website | Inbound inquiries from the right people every month |
| 3 | **Authority** | Book, speaking, podcast guesting, signature program, a team | Repeat demand, referrals, a waitlist or rising prices |
| 4 | **Leadership** | Own the space: events, academy, licensing the method, multiple platforms with owners | The method is known by name |

## Step 3: "When is the right time to ..."

| Asset | Build it when | Too early looks like | Minimum version first |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Website** | Stage 1 end / Stage 2: the claim is stable and there is an offer and some proof | Redesigning every quarter because the positioning keeps moving | A one-page site: claim, offer, proof, booking link. Full site at Stage 2. |
| **Book** | Stage 3: an audience exists, the framework has been taught many times, there are stories and results to fill it | Writing to "become an authority" with nobody to read it | Teach the book first as content and talks; the posts become chapters |
| **Podcast (own)** | Stage 2-3, with capacity for consistency | Episode 7 and stopping | Guest on other shows first (Stage 1-2) |
| **Speaking** | Stage 2-3: signature topics proven in content | Pitching stages with no clips | Record a talk, workshop, or live; clip it |
| **Course / academy** | Stage 3: the method works one-to-one repeatedly | A course nobody asked for | Run a live cohort or workshop first, then productize |
| **Paid ads** | Stage 2+: organic content has shown what converts | Paying to amplify an unclear message | Boost a proven organic post |
| **Team** | When a bottleneck blocks consistency (often editing, then a content lead) | Hiring before the process is documented | One freelancer on a paid trial project |
| **Rebrand / visual identity** | After Clarity and Ownership are locked | New logo to fix a positioning problem | Identity before design, always |
| **Business brand separate from personal** | When the business has its own offers and proof | Two empty accounts competing | Personal leads, business as a collab arm |

## Step 4: Goals output

```
LONG-TERM (12-36 months)   The Outcome, stated as a result
MID-TERM (90 days)         The current stage's gate
SHORT-TERM (30 days)       3-5 Actions, each owned, each measurable
NOT YET                    Assets deliberately postponed, and the gate that unlocks each
```

The "NOT YET" list matters. It protects the client from shiny objects and is
where most of the strategic value shows.
