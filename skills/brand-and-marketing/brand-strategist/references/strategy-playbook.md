# Phase 3 — The Strategy Playbook

Turning discovery answers into a brand. Built in sequence: Map -> Visual ->
Position -> Monetize -> Roadmap. Each step feeds the next. This is where the prose,
depth, and craft live.

> Method: **narrative-first.** Build structure *from* the client's story. Do not
> impose a template onto them; extract the architecture that's already in their
> answers, then name it.

---

## 4.1 Map — the Brand Backbone

The foundational document. Everything else references it. Sections:

1. **Core Identity** — who they are, distilled. Their story, refined into essence.
2. **Brand Essence** — the one-line soul of the brand.
3. **Brand Architecture** — how the parts relate (sub-brands, offers, platforms).
4. **Positioning Statement** — for [audience] who [need], [brand] is the [category]
   that [difference], because [reason to believe].
5. **Audience Definition** — the one person, their pain, what they're buying.
6. **Brand Voice** — how they sound, with do/don't examples in their own words.
7. **Vision Roadmap** — where this goes over time.
8. **Strategic Backbone** — the non-negotiables that hold it all together.

Write as flowing prose for a report. Tables only where they genuinely clarify
(e.g. offer tiers, voice do/don't). Ground claims in psychology *where it earns
its place* — post-traumatic growth, Social Identity Theory, Paul Zak on
storytelling, StoryBrand, mimetic theory (Thiel lens). Don't decorate with theory;
use it to deepen.

---

## 4.2 Visual / Expression Direction

Translate clarity into a visual system. Every choice ties back to an identity
truth from the map (expression is translation, not decoration).

- **Palette** — derived from the colour-world answers + the feeling they named.
- **Typography** — serif/sans logic matching their voice. The client gets their own pairing, never MonnyCreaX's Garamond/Lufga or another client's fonts.
- **Treatment** — photography style, graphic language, texture.
- **Content look** — the grid logic, how posts feel as a set.
- **The system** — rules so it stays consistent (the visibility fix in practice).

If building actual visuals, set the direction here and hand off to the design
skills (canvas-design, frontend-design) with this brief as input.

---

## 4.3 Positioning — the Claim (Ownership)

The single specific thing they own, stated without apology. This is where the
**hidden asset** from Phase 1 usually becomes the headline.

- **Positioning statement** — sharp, one sentence, defensible.
- **Messaging pillars** — 3-5 themes that prove the claim, each with talking
  points. These become content pillars downstream.
- **The enemy** — what they stand against, which sharpens what they stand for.

Ownership means leaving other spaces unclaimed. Make the position narrow enough to
be ownable, not broad enough to be invisible.

---

## 4.4 Monetization — the Offer Ladder

How the brand makes money, mapped as a ladder. Tie every rung to the audience's
real "what they're buying."

```
FREE        Content / lead magnet      -> builds trust, filters audience
ENTRY       Low-ticket / workshop      -> first transaction, low risk
CORE        Signature offer / service  -> the main engine
PREMIUM     High-touch / retainer      -> depth, transformation, recurring
```

**Pricing philosophy**: price the transformation, not the hours. Position as a
strategic partner, not a freelancer. Anchor with the premium tier so the core
reads as reasonable.

### The MonnyCreaX engagement / retainer model (reference)
A standard build runs as a **six-week engagement** moving through the CORE phases,
with an option to continue on **retainer** for ongoing expression and management.
Milestone-based where it suits the client. Use this as the template for service
offers, adapting scope and price to the client's level.

---

## 4.5 Roadmap — Execution

A sequenced plan. Nothing skipped, sequence matters.

### Milestone structure
```
M1  Analysis              "Where we are"        (the Phase 1 read, delivered)
M2  Social / Brand Refresh "How we show up"     (bio, profiles, visual fixes)
M3  Content / Video        "What we create"     (pillars + first content batch)
M4  SOPs & Structure       "How we operate"     (systems so it sustains)
```

### 30-Day Action Roadmap
Map week by week against CORE:
- **Week 1 — Clarity**: lock identity, audience, positioning.
- **Week 2 — Ownership**: claim the position publicly, update bios/profiles.
- **Week 3 — Realignment**: fix the gaps, align visuals and message.
- **Week 4 — Expression**: ship content on the new system, establish cadence.

End with the **content system**: the pillars (usually 5-6, derived from the
messaging pillars) and a starter cadence so the client can keep going without you.

---

## Phase 3 deliverables

Typically two pieces:
1. **Brand Report / Backbone** — the comprehensive flowing-prose document
   (can run 15-22+ pages). Internal-grade strategy, written to be readable.
2. **Brand Direction** — a shorter client-facing piece written directly *to* the
   client, warm and clear, translating the strategy into what it means for them.

Build both per the standards in SKILL.md section 4: landscape default, plain .docx
with ~30% theming unless full MonnyCreaX treatment is requested, type per SKILL.md section 4
(house fonts for MonnyCreaX documents, the client's own pairing for client brands), no em dashes, MonnyCreaX attribution, calm conviction-driven
voice with faith as a subtle throughline.
